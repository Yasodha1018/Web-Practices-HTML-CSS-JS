Flexbox Direction :
-------------------

it sets how flex items are places in the flex container, along which axis and direction.

- flexbox-direction: row(default)(in row in left to righ 1-n)--->
-flexbox-direction: row-reverse(in row but in right-left side 1-n)<---
-flexbox-direction:columns
-flexbox-direction-columns-reverse

flex properties :
------------------

for flex container
1. justify-content: alignment along the main axies .
flex-start/flex-end/center/space-evenly/space-between/
2. flex-wrap: nowrap/wrapwrap-reverse
3. align-items: alignment along the cross axis.
4. align-content: alignment of space between & around the content along cross - axis.


Flex properties:(for the items)
----------------------------------

1. align-self: alignment of individual along the cross axis.
2. flex-grow: how much a flex item will grow relative to 
the rest of the flex items if space is available.(grow equally in 1 )
3. flex-shrink: how much a flex item will shrink relative to the rest 
space is available.(auto shrink in equally 1 )
